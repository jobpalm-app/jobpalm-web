Support

Find answers for backups, purchases, and app data, or contact the developer directly.

Contact

Email jobpalm.support@proton.me. Include the app version and steps to reproduce. Do not include client details or a complete backup unless you intentionally choose to share them.

Back up your data

Open More → Backup and data → Export complete backup. Save the ZIP file somewhere you control. Complete backups are not password encrypted.

Restore Pro

Open More → JobPalm Pro → Restore purchases while signed in to the Apple Account that originally purchased Pro.

Delete local data

Open More → Backup and data → Erase all app data. This removes JobPalm business data stored by the app on that device, but it does not remove exported files or cancel an Apple purchase.

联系我们

请发送邮件至 jobpalm.support@proton.me。请附上 App 版本和问题复现步骤；除非你明确希望分享，否则请勿包含客户资料或完整备份。

备份数据

打开“更多”→“备份与数据”→“导出完整备份”，将 ZIP 文件保存在你能控制的位置。完整备份未使用密码加密。

恢复 Pro

使用最初购买 Pro 的 Apple 账户登录后，打开“更多”→“JobPalm Pro”→“恢复购买”。

删除本地数据

打开“更多”→“备份与数据”→“清除所有 App 数据”。这会删除该设备上由 JobPalm 保存的业务数据，但不会删除已导出的文件，也不会取消 Apple 内购。
