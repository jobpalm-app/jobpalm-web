Support

Practical help for capture, review, reports, purchases, and local data.

Contact

Email support@zqforge.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, FieldPalm version, what you expected, and the steps that reproduced the issue. Do not include customer evidence unless necessary and authorized.

Getting started

Create a Job, enter a unique Job number and service type, then start it. In Capture, select Before, Work, After, Issue, or Equipment before taking each photo. Review equipment details, Before/After matches, closeout text, and signatures before confirming them.

Camera or voice is unavailable

Open iOS Settings → Apps → FieldPalm and allow only the permissions you want to use. Confirm the device has free storage. Typing remains available when microphone, speech recognition, or supported on-device intelligence is unavailable.

Reports and exports

Generate reports from a completed closeout. Each generated revision is preserved. Use Share only when you are ready to send the PDF or evidence ZIP to a destination you control.

Restore Pro

Open Settings → Manage Plan → Restore Purchases while signed in to the Apple Account that originally purchased FieldPalm Pro. Existing local Jobs are not deleted if Pro becomes unavailable.

Local data

FieldPalm has no account or cloud sync. Keep the app installed and maintain an appropriate device backup. Deleting a Job or uninstalling FieldPalm can remove local records; exported files must be managed separately.
