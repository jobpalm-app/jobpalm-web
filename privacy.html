Privacy Policy

JobPalm is designed as a local-first workspace. Your business records stay in the app on your iPhone unless you choose to export or share them.

No JobPalm accountThe app does not require registration or a JobPalm cloud account.
No ads or trackingThere are no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs in the app.
You control exportsBackups, reports, PDFs, and messages leave the app only when you initiate them.

Effective date: September 3, 2026
Applies to: the JobPalm iPhone app

1. Overview

This policy explains how the JobPalm app handles information. “We” and “JobPalm” refer to the developer of the JobPalm app. JobPalm does not require an account and does not operate a server that receives your business library.

2. Information stored on your device

You may enter a business profile, client names and contact details, service addresses, visits, estimates, payment records, service notes, and photos. This information is stored in JobPalm’s local app container on your iPhone. The developer does not receive this information through the app.

3. Photos and permissions

JobPalm requests access only when you choose a related feature:

  • Photos and camera: You choose photos to import or explicitly open the camera. JobPalm stores a resized JPEG copy and removes unnecessary metadata, including GPS metadata. Your original photo-library items are not modified.
  • Notifications: If you enable reminders, JobPalm schedules local notifications for you. Private previews are the default; showing client names is optional. JobPalm does not contact your clients.

4. Apple services and network access

JobPalm may interact with Apple services for limited app functions:

  • Apple processes optional Pro purchases through StoreKit. JobPalm receives verified purchase status but does not receive your payment-card details.
  • The app may contact Apple’s App Store lookup service to check whether a newer JobPalm version is available.
  • If you choose Directions, JobPalm opens Apple Maps with the selected service address. Apple handles that request under its own privacy policy.

JobPalm contains no advertising SDK and no third-party analytics SDK.

5. Backups, exports, and sharing

You decide whether to export a backup, CSV report, estimate PDF, or message through system interfaces. Full backups are ZIP files and are not password encrypted. They can include client details, financial records, notes, and supported attachments. Store them only in locations you trust.

Device backups, cloud file providers, Apple services, and apps you select in the system share sheet may process information under their own policies. JobPalm cannot verify delivery to a recipient or erase copies outside the app’s container.

6. Purchases and recorded payments

Apple handles the optional Pro in-app purchase. Payment records you create inside JobPalm are notes about transactions made outside the app; JobPalm does not initiate, receive, or transfer client funds.

7. Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you edit or erase them. After a restore, a previous local library may remain as a recovery point until you delete it. The in-app erase action removes JobPalm business libraries, attachments, recovery points, temporary exports, and pending JobPalm reminders from the device.

Erasing app data does not cancel an Apple purchase or remove files you already exported, copies held by recipients, or system backups. Uninstalling the app normally removes its local container, subject to Apple’s device and backup behavior.

8. Support communications

If you email support, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address, app version, and message. Do not send client details or a complete backup unless you intentionally choose to share that content. Support correspondence is kept only as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain service records, and meet applicable obligations. You may request deletion by contacting us.

9. This website

This privacy-policy page does not add JobPalm analytics or advertising cookies. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, so GitHub may process technical request information according to the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when JobPalm’s features or data handling change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when a revised policy is published.

Contact

Questions, privacy requests, or deletion requests can be sent to jobpalm.support@proton.me.

隐私政策

JobPalm 采用本地优先设计。除非你主动导出或分享，业务资料会保存在 iPhone 上的 App 内。

无需 JobPalm 账号无需注册，也无需创建 JobPalm 云端账号。
无广告与跟踪App 不包含广告 SDK 或第三方分析 SDK。
导出由你控制只有你主动操作时，备份、报表、PDF 或消息才会离开 App。

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 3 日
适用范围：JobPalm iPhone App

1. 概述

本政策说明 JobPalm App 如何处理信息。“我们”和“JobPalm”指 JobPalm App 的开发者。JobPalm 不要求注册账号，也不运营用于接收你的业务资料库的服务器。

2. 保存在设备上的信息

你可以录入商家资料、客户姓名与联系方式、服务地址、服务日程、报价、收款记录、服务备注和照片。这些信息保存在 iPhone 上 JobPalm 的本地 App 容器中，开发者不会通过 App 收到这些信息。

3. 照片与权限

只有当你选择相关功能时，JobPalm 才会请求对应权限：

  • 照片与相机：由你选择导入照片或主动打开相机。JobPalm 会保存缩小后的 JPEG 副本，并移除包括 GPS 在内的不必要元数据，不会修改系统相册中的原始照片。
  • 通知：启用提醒后，JobPalm 会为你安排本地通知。默认使用私密预览，你可以选择是否显示客户姓名。JobPalm 不会联系你的客户。

4. Apple 服务与网络访问

JobPalm 可能在有限的 App 功能中使用 Apple 服务：

  • 可选的 Pro 内购由 Apple 通过 StoreKit 处理。JobPalm 会读取经过验证的购买状态，但不会收到你的银行卡资料。
  • App 可能访问 Apple 的 App Store 查询服务，以检查 JobPalm 是否有新版本。
  • 当你选择“路线”时，JobPalm 会使用选定的服务地址打开 Apple 地图，该请求由 Apple 按其隐私政策处理。

JobPalm 不包含广告 SDK 或第三方分析 SDK。

5. 备份、导出与分享

是否通过系统界面导出完整备份、CSV 报表、报价 PDF 或消息由你决定。完整备份为未使用密码加密的 ZIP 文件，其中可能包含客户信息、财务记录、备注和支持的附件。请仅将其保存在你信任的位置。

设备备份、云端文件服务商、Apple 服务以及你在系统分享菜单中选择的 App，可能依据各自的政策处理信息。JobPalm 无法验证收件人是否收到内容，也无法删除 App 容器以外的副本。

6. 内购与收款记录

可选的 Pro App 内购买由 Apple 处理。你在 JobPalm 中创建的收款记录，只是对 App 外部交易的记录；JobPalm 不会发起、接收或转移客户资金。

7. 保存与删除

本地记录会保留到你修改或清除为止。恢复备份后，先前的本地资料库可能作为恢复点保留，直到你主动删除。App 内的清除操作会从设备中移除 JobPalm 业务资料库、附件、恢复点、临时导出文件以及尚未触发的 JobPalm 提醒。

清除 App 数据不会取消 Apple 内购，也不会删除已经导出的文件、收件人保存的副本或系统备份。卸载 App 通常会移除其本地容器，具体仍受 Apple 的设备与备份机制影响。

8. 支持沟通

如果你向支持邮箱发送邮件，我们会收到你主动提供的信息，例如邮箱地址、App 版本和邮件内容。除非你明确希望分享，否则请勿发送客户资料或完整备份。支持邮件只会在回复问题、保留必要服务记录和履行适用义务所需的合理期限内保存。你可以联系我们申请删除。

9. 本网站

本隐私政策网页未添加 JobPalm 分析工具或广告 Cookie。网站由 GitHub Pages 托管，因此 GitHub 可能依据其隐私声明处理技术请求信息。

10. 政策变更

当 JobPalm 的功能或数据处理方式发生变化时，我们可能更新本政策。发布修订版本时，页面顶部的生效日期也会更新。
