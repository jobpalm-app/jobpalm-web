## First-user recruitment when you do not know where to look

Do not ask broadly for “beta testers.” Ask people with a specific workflow problem. Start with one occupation at a time: cleaners, gardeners, mobile pet groomers, tutors, repair technicians, or other appointment-based solo operators.

### Direct recruitment message

Hi — I built an iPhone app for independent service professionals who currently manage clients and visits with Notes, Calendar, or spreadsheets. I am looking for five people willing to use it for one real workweek and tell me where it slows them down. It is free to try, does not require a JobPalm account, and I am not asking for client data. Would you be open to a 15-minute feedback chat after trying it?

### 中文招募文案

你好，我做了一款 iPhone App，帮助独立服务从业者管理客户、上门安排、报价和收款记录，适合目前还在用备忘录、日历或表格的人。我正在找 5 位愿意在真实工作中试用一周、并告诉我哪里不好用的用户。App 可以免费体验，不需要注册 JobPalm 账户，也不需要向我提供任何客户资料。你愿意试用后花 15 分钟反馈一下吗？

### Where to post first

1. One local trade or small-business WeChat group where you are already a genuine member.
2. Reddit communities for a single occupation, following each community’s self-promotion rules.
3. Facebook groups for local solo service businesses, with a feedback request rather than a sales pitch.
4. LinkedIn posts aimed at independent service operators, showing one real workflow instead of a feature list.
5. Ask one accountant, equipment supplier, or coworking-space operator to introduce you to two suitable businesses.

### Five-question feedback checklist

1. What did you use before JobPalm?
2. Which real task did you try first?
3. Where did you hesitate or get stuck?
4. What information was missing or unnecessary?
5. Would you keep using it next week? Why or why not?

Do not request screenshots or exported backups containing client information. Ask users to describe the workflow or reproduce it with non-sensitive sample data.
